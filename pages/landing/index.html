How it works

One order, every screen, zero re-typing.

01

Order

At a kiosk, counter, captain device, your app, online or a delivery platform.

02

Pay

NFC card, card, UPI, wallet or cash, through any integrated gateway.

03

Token

A token number is issued and printed on the receipt.

04

Cook

The ticket lands on the KDS instantly. Cooks bump when done.

05

Collect

The CDS flips the token to Ready and the app notifies the guest.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Ordobit only for restaurants?

No. Ordobit is built for any food service operation: corporate cafeterias, college and school canteens, hospital and factory canteens, multi-vendor food courts, cafés, QSRs and restaurants.

How do NFC cashless payments work?

Each user gets an NFC wallet card that is topped up at a counter or online. They tap it at any kiosk or counter on the site, and balances, limits and history are managed centrally.

Can one platform run a food court with many vendors?

Yes. Each vendor gets its own menu, KDS and reports, while the site operator sees all sales in one place and settles with each vendor.

Can customers order from our own app?

Yes. We set up a branded customer app for your business. Orders placed in it go straight to your Ordobit POS and KDS, where staff accept and process them like any other order.

Do you integrate with third-party ordering platforms?

Yes. Orders from delivery and aggregator platforms flow into the same POS, so you don't need a separate tablet per platform. Tell us which platforms you use and we'll confirm availability for your region.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Ordobit runs on standard Windows and Android devices. If you prefer a ready-to-go setup, we can bundle kiosks, POS terminals, printers and displays.

Which payment methods are supported?

NFC wallets, cards, UPI, QR, digital wallets and cash, through multiple gateway integrations. Tell us your region and we'll confirm the options.

Can you build custom features for us?

Yes. We build custom applications and integrations for enterprises, campuses and chains, such as HR or ERP sync, subsidy rules and access control.
